Engineering · Mid-level

WordPress Developer

Full-time Karachi / Hybrid 3–5 years

We’re looking for a mid-level WordPress developer who can own theme and plugin work end to end—clean builds, maintainable code, and sites that perform under real traffic. You’ll work closely with design and project leads on client websites, landing pages, and content-driven platforms.

What you’ll do

  • Build and customize WordPress themes (block / classic) from approved designs.
  • Develop or extend plugins and integrations (forms, CRM, payments, analytics).
  • Implement responsive, accessible front-end with solid HTML, CSS, and modern JS.
  • Optimize performance, SEO basics, and Core Web Vitals on client sites.
  • Handle staging, deployments, and post-launch fixes with clear communication.

What you bring

  • 3–5 years of professional WordPress development experience.
  • Strong PHP, theme architecture, hooks/filters, and custom post types / ACF.
  • Comfortable with WooCommerce or page builders when the project needs them.
  • Git-based workflow, staging discipline, and attention to security basics.
  • Clear written English and the habit of documenting what you ship.

Nice to have: headless WordPress, Laravel familiarity, or agency client delivery experience.

Engineering · Mid-level

Mobile App Developer — Flutter

Full-time Karachi / Hybrid 3–5 years

We’re hiring a mid-level Flutter developer to build and ship production iOS and Android apps. You’ll turn product requirements into clean Dart architecture, reliable API integration, and polished UI—owning features from planning through store-ready release.

What you’ll do

  • Develop cross-platform Flutter apps with maintainable Dart and clear state management.
  • Integrate REST/GraphQL APIs, auth flows, push notifications, and third-party SDKs.
  • Implement responsive UI from design specs with attention to performance and edge cases.
  • Write solid tests, debug production issues, and improve app stability over time.
  • Support App Store / Play Console releases, versioning, and store listing updates.

What you bring

  • 3–5 years of mobile development, with strong recent Flutter / Dart delivery.
  • Experience with Provider, Riverpod, Bloc, or GetX—and knowing when each fits.
  • Familiarity with Firebase, local storage, and secure handling of user data.
  • Comfortable with Git, CI basics, and collaborating with designers and backend.
  • A portfolio or shipped apps you can walk through in an interview.

Nice to have: native iOS/Android exposure, maps/payments modules, or publishing apps end to end.
